RESEARCH DECISION NOTES — 30 October 2026

Measured candidate bottleneck: BM25 top10 contained all reference evidence for
16/24 questions. Its rerank-only methods cannot exceed that ceiling. Phase-based
query expansion retrieves from the full eligible corpus and reaches 19/24.
This points to candidate generation/link traversal rather than ranking alone.
It is a development observation, not a new algorithm or proof of generalization.

Changing a model can be a useful baseline experiment. A model substitution alone
does not establish a research contribution. This pilot does not reproduce a
published system, and cannot establish a win over one.

Candidate problem: reduce undetected mistakes when applying chains of regulatory
amendments, while limiting the fraction requiring human review. A candidate
implementation predicts localized edits and checks target uniqueness, preservation
of untouched text, dates, and dependency validity. Reject uncertain edits. These
components are NOT claimed novel; structured amendment execution is longstanding.
The potential contribution would require a specific new method and convincing
held-out evaluation beyond these standard controls.

Primary paper to investigate/reproduce:
Prior, Hof, Wais, Grabmair (NLLP 2025), Risks and Limits of Automatic Consolidation
of Statutes. https://aclanthology.org/2025.nllp-1.29/
908 amendment-law pairs across four German codes; reported exact match approximately
50.3% single-step and 20.51% multi-step. Uses GPT-4.1 for amendment extraction and
GPT-4.1-mini for application. These are DIFFERENT metrics/data from our retrieval
pilot. Cannot compare our 72/72 rule lookup or retrieval score against them.
Sources are public, but availability of their assembled dataset/code has NOT been
confirmed. Reproduction feasibility remains a gate. German legal assessment also
requires suitable language expertise. No claim that this is easy to beat.

Additional overlap:
https://lawvm.org/ describes deterministic amendment replay, point-in-time text,
unresolved operations and verification. This is project documentation, not a
peer-reviewed accuracy evaluation. It nonetheless prevents presenting those
engineering features as automatically original.
https://github.com/opnsrcntrbtr/sebi-circular-rag/blob/main/docs/status.md
describes SEBI retrieval with hybrid search, reranking, lineage, temporal queries
and abstention. Self-reported metrics were not independently verified. Domain
choice plus these features is not an adequate novelty claim.
https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12599-026-01008-x
Taming the Complexity of Legal Change for Business Process Compliance (2026)
reviews legal-change work and proposes an LLM approach for compliance impact.

Proposed commitment gate, NOT completed experiments:
1. Confirm accessible data and reproducible baseline; establish a separate test
   split by amendment chain/document family, not random questions from one chain.
2. Compare direct rewriting, standard structured-edit execution, deterministic
   checks, and the proposed improvement using the same backbone and inputs.
3. Report exact match, errors in unchanged clauses, chain-length degradation,
   and error versus coverage/review burden. Abstentions cannot count as successes.
4. Remove components to establish what causes any gain. Compare resource costs.
5. Independently review labels and severity. Only then assess publication fit.

No hospital/private data is needed for this legal-document direction. Public
documents still require reliable reference annotations and evaluation design.
