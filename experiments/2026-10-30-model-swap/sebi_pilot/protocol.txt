SEBI micro-pilot, fixed before scoring, 30 October 2026

This is an exploratory feasibility test on two selected regulatory families,
not a 20-chain study, independent legal validation, or a reproduction of any
published RAG system. The developer has read all source documents. No held-out
family exists; source-specific parsing choices are development work.

Corpus: eight official SEBI PDFs: 2020 IA guidelines; January 2025 IA and RA
guidelines; April 2025 advance fee amendment; January, February, July and
December 2025 nomination circulars. Historical closed-corpus questions only.
No assertion that this corpus exhausts SEBI law, or describes current rules.

Tasks:
1. Evidence retrieval: BM25, MiniLM dense embeddings, reciprocal-rank hybrid,
publication-date-filtered hybrid. Evaluate required-source-page recall and
all-required-pages at k=3 and 5. Page granularity is a practical micro-pilot
choice, not a tuned reproduction of clause-level systems. Dense model truncates
pages to 512 tokens; report this limitation.
2. Structured rule resolution: a common source-derived fact representation,
with fields, entities, scopes and source pages, used by every selector.
Compare relevance-only selection, publication-date-filtered selection, latest
applicable field selection, and phase-aware resolution with scope handling.
Include a simple effective-dated table control with phase updates flattened
into clause-level facts. If it ties the resolver, report no architectural gain.
No LLM generator is evaluated. Scores are structured-field accuracy, not
end-to-end RAG answer accuracy or legal correctness certification.

Gold: explicit manually authored expected field values and evidence-page sets,
checked against source text/table image by the assistant, not an independent
legal expert. Generated date/phrase variants are correlated; report counts
and family breakdown, no significance claim from treating them as independent.
Question slots (date/entity/clause/field/scope) are supplied to all structured
selectors. This isolates resolution and does not test natural-language parsing.

Nomination questions ask the ANNOUNCED implementation schedule as known on a
specified date, not retrospective enforceability. Publication chronology is
therefore appropriate. Do not reinterpret a July postponement as having been
known in June. Gold unknown/deferred is a valid output. Unchanged clauses
inherit their original schedule. Require table membership plus later phase
update evidence for clause-specific changes. Fee questions restrict to new
clients to avoid transitional agreement ambiguity; scope exceptions tested.

Record data/source hashes, model revision, full per-question predictions,
retrieved pages, primary summaries and failure examples. Before first score,
freeze gold and code hashes. Fix implementation defects if necessary but keep
first-run results and document corrections. Do not tune against resulting scores.

Go/no-go: whether the real-source task is executable and whether a specialized
resolver improves over a simple effective-dated field table, not whether it
beats naive retrieval. Any oracle/manual normalization advantage must be named.

Pre-score execution contingency: the MiniLM weight endpoint is timing out.
Run BM25, date-filtered BM25 and explicitly named TF-IDF sparse control even
if dense weights remain unavailable. Do not label TF-IDF as dense or a hybrid
reproduction. If weights become available, add planned dense/hybrid scores
without changing questions, source parser, selection rules or hyperparameters.
